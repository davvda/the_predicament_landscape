Rest


How can it be that so many of us have never done an honest day of work yet we're exhausted, in desperate need of rest? We need time away from the frantic pedalling, endless churning, progress, development, connectivity, all aspects of modern life, restlessness. Things happen in rest that do not happen in motion. Similarly to how abs are made in the kitchen, not in the gym, constant toil with no rest leads to nowhere desirable. Humanity, the engines of modernity, and the individuals making all of it up need a global sabbath, some well- or ill-deserved rest. Resting is resisting. In our striving to become Gods we've forgotten that even God rested.

My notion of heaven is my family safely resting in our nest, sun cats shimmering on the living room floor, ambient music quietly playing through the speakers. Rest and know that all is well.
