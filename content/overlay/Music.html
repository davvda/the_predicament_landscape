Music

Music Textures Time

As visual art textures space, music textures time. In Western musical theory we often speak of three core elements: melody, harmony, and rythm. These three work together with pitch, timbre, texture, volume, duration, and form. All blend into sounds which sculpt and texture time. What is music or not depends on both the creator or capturer of sounds as well as the listener. All sounds can be musical and all music can be perceived as just sounds.

Music as such is not exclusively human but the ways in which we use music may very well be. For as long as we have been humans we have had rythm in our blood, harmonising people intent on a joint purpose, singing while tilling fields, beating marching war drums, telling stories, praising the gods, protesting against the rulers, combating the oncoming dark of night, lamenting the dead, welcoming the newborn, we seem ever bent to put our mark on lived time. In a world full of dissonant choirs, do your part to return our song to harmony.

---

Pick up an instrument, sing, alone or with others, learn to play something, if you aren't good just reject any preconceived notions of what music is or should be. Make music. Also, listen to music, alone or with others. Discover music, explore, find what resonates, maybe literally, let music fill your life, find music in silence, find silence in music, listen, listen close, listen broadly, everything is music, music is everything. Sing, sing as if your life depended on it because it does.
