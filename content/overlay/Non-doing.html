Non-doing

In Zen practice there is the concept of shikantaza, just sitting, a meditation practice emphasising the non-doing aspect of zazen, seated meditation. Just sit. In Taoism one frequently speaks of wu wei, non-doing or effortless action. A way of summarising it might be that non-doing is impossibly difficult for us and requires effortful practice even if both the path and result should be fully effortless non-doing.

There really is nothing that needs doing. Focus all your attention and inattention at non-doing and you will leave nothing undone.

Ill-being, the causes of ill-being, the end of Ill-being, the path, insight and attainment, are also not separate self entities. Whoever can see this no longer needs anything to attain.

- Excerpt from the Heart Sutra, translation by Thich Nhat Hanh
