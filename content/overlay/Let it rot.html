Let it rot

The whole time this society is telling me and training me to be a rocket but when I'm actually out there it's so dark, so lonely, and I just want to draw a complete circle under my feet

- Anonymous Chinese youth

The Chinese phrase bai lan, "to actively embrace a deteriorating situation, rather than trying to turn it around", or more simply, "letting it rot" has taken hold of much of Chinese youth, disillusioned with the dreams and promises clearly impossible to fulfill. Tell me, are you traversing this landscape with the intention and hope of turning it all around? Surely, we can find a compromise or even a win-win where the best parts of modern society can be saved while the worst is cast off? The core of modernity was always a stillborn in clown makeup, nip off a piece of it, keep it as a dormant sourdough starter, actively embrace the deterioration of the rest, let it rot, steer for more fertile pastures, you know where some can be found, and if not, this landscape can point you in the right direction.

On a more personal note, many with my disposition have probably found themselves working with or at least striving to partake in the field of "sustainability". Now, I will not tell you to give up your grand ambitions. All I ask is that you are clear with yourself and others what exactly it is you are working to sustain. I have not yet seen a single sustainability project whose stated intent and direct actions aren't at least partially in conflict. Just let it rot.
