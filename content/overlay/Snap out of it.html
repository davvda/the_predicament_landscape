Snap out of it

There is too much bad news to justify complacency. There is too much good news to justify despair.

- Donella Meadows

Screen lights up your face, blank stare of stupour, thin-skinned, insecure, fretful, inbred furless pugs struck dumb by the howling moon. Fucking snap out of it. Do you see yourself? Simmering away in a boiling sea of ways, all you have to do is something, anything, your wheezing cough adds nothing but another gob of phlegm in the pit of doom. Think, act, care, speak, listen, see, really see, anything beats nothing, snap out of it.
