Allow for recovery


Fuck the good Samaritan. There are times when the best thing to do about something is to leave it be. Active interventions to heal are often veiled attempts at control, albeit in new and different ways. Allow places to recover. Leave it to nature, often better abled to handle the consequences of our disruptive actions than we are ourselves. Time doesn't heal all wounds but time disturbed by the very thing which harmed you heals nothing. We aren't always needed. Take a bit of humble pie and shut up. And you, Samaritan, mediocre at best, walk on by, you aren't as benevolent or gracious as you'd have us believe.
