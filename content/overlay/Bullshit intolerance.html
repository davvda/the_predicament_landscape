Bullshit intolerance

When philosopher Ivan Illich was asked how he could've been so wrong about humanity's response or lack thereof to the over-institutionalisation of society, he replied that he did not believe that "so many people could be so tolerant of nonsense". How tolerant are you of nonsense? How much utter bullshit is allowed into your life, unnoticed, or noticed but ignored? If you believe the amount to be very little, you are probably the bullshitter.

If you haven't begun already I implore you to develop an intolerance to bullshit. Call it out. Do not stand idly by and let the shit flow unimpeded.
