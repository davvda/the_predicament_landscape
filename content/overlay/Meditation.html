Meditation


Meditation is the deliberate practice of achieving certain states of being, frequently under simplified circumstances. For humans, it is obligatory. Fix your hearts or die.
Sit upright and comfortably. Breathe in whichever way feels natural. Close your eyes or keep them half-open. Stay upright, do not slouch. If you feel agitated or heavy-minded, see if you can find ease by taking a few deeper breaths, ten or twenty might do. Notice your body. Notice your breath. If it helps you focus, count your breaths. Inhale, one, exhale, one, inhale, two, exhale, two, etc. Up to ten, back to one. Upright, do not slouch. Thoughts arise, you lose focus, gently acknowledge that thinking happened, return to the breath. That is all. It is difficult, simple, and effective. It doesn't get easier, only subtler. Practice regularly, longer sessions are better but anything beats nothing by miles. It may feel like it changes nothing, then suddenly, gradually, you find it changes everything.
