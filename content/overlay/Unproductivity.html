Unproductivity

How much of your time is spent trying to be or worrying about not being productive? We all need time when we're allowed to be wholly unproductive. Keep in mind that most of our so-called unproductive time has been captured by capitalism via the freedom to endlessly consume. Passive consumption is still largely a productive act. Find time and places outside of capitalism, commerce, and production, leave the wheel for a peaceful moment of utter unproductivity.
