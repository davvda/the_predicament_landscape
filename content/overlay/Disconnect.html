Disconnect

I am losing precious days. I am degenerating into a machine for making money. I am learning nothing in this trivial world of men. I must break away and get out into the mountains to learn the news.

- John Muir

Unplug, don the foilhat, enter the Faraday cage. There is nothing to keep up with, you do not need to stay informed, leave the unceasing stream, for it is nothing but a vortex feeding off of itself. Concretely and unfortunately somehow more realistic, make an effort to find opportunities to disconnect from any information network you're connected to. TV, the internet, news, social media, politics, gossip, just find moments to put it all on mute and walk away. If you must, tune your antennae to the grapevine. The revolution will not be televised.
